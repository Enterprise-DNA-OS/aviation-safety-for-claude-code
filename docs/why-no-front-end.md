# Why there is no front end

SMS Pro is a database with a subscription. The records underneath it are ordinary: reports, hazards, risk ratings, actions, audits, indicators, training. What you pay for is the layer on top that lets people who do not write queries get at them: forms, registers, dashboards.

That layer used to be the whole product, because talking to a database was hard. It is not hard any more. Open this folder in Claude Code, describe what you want, and it runs the right query and explains the answer. Ask a question the dashboard never had a chart for and you still get an answer.

## What you gain

- **Better answers.** Which hazards are tolerable with nothing being done about them? Which base reports least per 1,000 hours? You ask in your own words.
- **No headcount pricing.** Every pilot, engineer and ground crew member can be in the records. The bill does not grow with the operation.
- **Your data in your Postgres.** Plain tables. Back them up, query them from anything, leave any time.
- **A system that matches your exposition.** Your matrix, your categories, your house rules, changed by asking.

## What a screen gives that this does not

- **A reporting form on every phone.** Staff here report through the safety manager, by email or by asking Claude Code. A one-tap phone form is the most common thing an operator adds, and Enterprise DNA builds it on the same records.
- **Offline use.** It runs where Claude Code runs.
- **Drag and drop.** The hazard register is a table you ask about, not cards you move.
- **A vendor help desk.** This is open source. Enterprise DNA supports the installed version for operators who want someone to call.

## Who this fits

A safety manager who already uses Claude Code, or would rather ask than learn another interface. If your whole team needs a screen to file reports today, have the phone form built before you switch.
